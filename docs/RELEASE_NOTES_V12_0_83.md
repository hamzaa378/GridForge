# GridForge v12.0.83

## Box & Whisker chart options

- Choose inclusive or exclusive quartile calculations.
- Toggle inner observations and outlier markers independently.
- Show a mean marker and optionally connect means across series.
- Preserve the settings in native Excel ChartEx `visibility` and `statistics` metadata on export and import.
